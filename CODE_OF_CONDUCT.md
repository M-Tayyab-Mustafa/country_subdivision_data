# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate,
welcome constructive disagreement, and focus feedback on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and sustained
disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may clarify standards, moderate contributions, and remove
participants whose conduct is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement decisions should be
fair, proportionate, and documented for maintainers.
